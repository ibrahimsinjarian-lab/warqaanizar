about

I want to invite people into my own world.

Warqaa Nazar is a writer and designer based between language and space. Her practice moves across essays, architectural thinking, and interior work, each exploring how the places we inhabit carry meaning we did not put there and meaning we urgently need to add.

The questions that drive her work are the same across every medium. How does a space say who belongs inside it? What does a room owe the person living in it? How does design become a site for equality or for its opposite?

She is interested in ideas that stick. In work that does not just sit on a wall or a page but changes the way someone moves through the world after encountering it.
